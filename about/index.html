About Me

Hello, I'm Shambhavi.

I’m a writer, aspiring filmmaker, and creative storyteller drawn to the things that make us deeply human — love, longing, memory, identity, femininity, and the quiet complexities of interpersonal relationships.

I’ve always been fascinated by art in all its forms. Books, poetry, cinema, music, fashion, photography; anything that speaks honestly to the human heart has a way of staying with me. At the same time, I’ve always had an equal appreciation for logic and analysis. I enjoy mathematics, research, problem-solving, and learning languages, and I find beauty in both emotion and structure.

This website was created as a space to bring all parts of my creativity together. For a long time, my work existed scattered across different platforms, mediums, and unfinished ideas. Here, I wanted to build something more intentional, a home for my writing, photography, thoughts, and visual storytelling.

Through essays, images, and future film work, I hope to create things that feel intimate, reflective, and emotionally honest.

I studied computer science, though over time I found myself increasingly drawn toward creative work and storytelling. Right now, I’m exploring what it means to bridge both worlds (analytical thinking and artistic expression) while building a life centered around meaningful creation.

Thank you for being here. I hope something on this site resonates with you.
