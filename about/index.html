About Me

Creative storyteller and technologist with an eye for the beautiful and meaningful.

Hello, I'm Shambhavi.

I’m a creative technologist exploring how ideas can take shape across different mediums—from interfaces and mobile apps to film and writing. With a background in computer science, I started out building functional systems, but became increasingly drawn to the expressive side of creation—how things feel, not just how they work.

My work moves between code and storytelling, often blending the two. Whether I’m designing a digital product or creating a visual narrative, I’m interested in crafting experiences that are intuitive, engaging, and emotionally resonant.

I work with a range of tools across development, design, and media—using code as one medium among many, alongside film, writing, and visual curation. This multidisciplinary approach shapes how I think: not in terms of platforms, but in terms of ideas and how they can be translated.

Outside of my work, I’m drawn to fashion, film, and visual culture, and I spend time creating and curating—through YouTube, photography, and writing. I’m always exploring new ways to tell stories, experiment with form, and push ideas further.
